Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Clip Poster ("the app") is a private tool the account owner uses to upload video clips to their own TikTok account. This policy explains what data it handles.

1. Data the app receives from TikTok

  • Basic profile information (TikTok user ID, display name) from the user.info.basic scope, used only to confirm which account is signed in.
  • An access token and refresh token, used only to upload videos to that account.

2. How it is stored

Tokens and profile information are stored only on the account owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server run by the app, and the app has no database or website backend.

3. How it is used

The data is used only to upload the account owner's own video clips to their TikTok account. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or tracking.

4. Data sent to TikTok

When a clip is uploaded, the video file and its caption are sent to TikTok and handled under TikTok's Privacy Policy.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens remain on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them or revokes access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. Revoking access stops the app from uploading anything further.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and is used only by its adult owner.
